## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-24

The first release. 📺 *"Good evening, creatures of the night, and welcome to the Channel 13 Creature Feature."*

### The Creature Feature

- An interactive explorer hosted by Count Cathode, with a TV-static opening, colours for each monster and a gallery of the whole vault
- A page for every monster with Facts, Legend, The Film, Myth vs Movie (guess, then press `r` for the verdicts), Quotes from the public-domain novels, a Stat Card and Legacy
- A black-and-white silent-film mode, with title cards and flickering grain, for the Phantom and the Hunchback
- `/` search across every monster's names, facts, films and legends
- Jump straight to a monster with `monster <name>`: nicknames and partial names work too
- Layouts that adapt from 40-column terminals to wide screens

### Games

- **The Midnight Quiz** (`quiz`): questions built from the monster data, each with an explanation, ending with a rank from Ghoul-in-Training to Master of Horror
- **Guess the Monster** (`guess`): name the monster as its portrait clears from the fog
- **The Crypt** (`crypt`): capture a monster by answering three questions about it, and earn 14 badges
- **The Monster Mash** (`mash`): three-round bouts decided by stat cards and a roll of the dice

### Nightly rituals

- `random --daily`: a Fact of the Night that's the same for everyone all day, made for your shell's startup file
- `tonight`: a retro double-feature ticket that changes every night
- `countdown`: the nights until Halloween, with the 31 Nights of Fright throughout October
- Full moons, Friday the 13th and classic film anniversaries, all worked out offline, plus badges for playing on those nights

### Monsters

- 19 monsters: 12 Universal Classics and 7 from world folklore (the Golem, the Banshee, Baba Yaga, the Kappa, the Jiangshi, La Llorona and Krampus)
- Film credits, folklore background, myth-vs-movie checks, powers, weaknesses and stats for each
- Corrected facts, e.g. the 1941 Wolf Man is killed with a silver-headed cane, not a silver bullet

### Community packs

- `packs new <id>` creates your own pack of monsters, no Go required
- `--pack` limits any command to chosen packs

### For scripting and contributors

- `--json` output for `list`, `random`, `monster`, `quiz`, `crypt` and `packs`
- `TERMINAL_OF_TERROR_HOME` moves saved progress and community packs; `TERMINAL_OF_TERROR_SEED` makes every random choice repeatable
- CI on Linux, macOS and Windows, with a smoke test of every command
- Demo GIFs and screenshots in the README, re-recorded with `docs/demos/render.sh`
- Release binaries for Linux, macOS and Windows (Intel and ARM)
